# 00 Context: the challenge and our constraints

## The programme (as read on gnani.ai/internship and public posts; re-check the page)
- Great Indian AI Internship Challenge by Gnani AI. Build something useful with Gnani's models/APIs, record a working demo, post it on **LinkedIn or X** with `#GnaniAI` and `#GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge`, and submit before **10 Nov 2026, 11:59 PM IST**.
- Solo or **team of two**. Registration gives **5,000 programme credits** (check whose account holds them).
- Models: **Prisma** (speech-to-text, v2.5 and v3.0), **Timbre** (text-to-speech, v2.5 and v3.0; TTS marked beta in posts), **Evon v3.3** (30B open-weight model, Apache-2.0, you run it yourself).
- Selection: internship shortlisting is based on **verified public engagement** on the nominated demo post, then one-on-one interviews. Teammates are interviewed separately. Bought engagement, bots and engagement exchanges are prohibited.
- Awards: 10 x ₹50,000, independent of internships; one award per participant. Categories: Main Character (regional/low-resource language), Mixed Vibes (code-mixed), Real Deal (farmers/kirana/rural health), Noise Canceller, Two-Person Army, Solo Ninja, Jugaad Genius, Small Town Big Build (Tier-2/3), Demo Day Drop (60-second demo), Crowd Favourite.
- Data rule: use **synthetic data**. No real phone numbers, account numbers, Aadhaar, PAN or recorded calls in the project or demo.
- Internships: 6 months from 1 Jan 2027, stipend up to ₹1 lakh/month (performance/interview based).

## Our positioning
Primary candidate categories: **Main Character** (Bengali) and **Jugaad Genius**; also Mixed Vibes, Two-Person Army, Demo Day Drop. Choose the one we submit for at the end, based on the strongest finished work.

## What the crowd looks like (sample of ~55 LinkedIn/X posts, Oct-Nov 2026; partial)
Crowded: kirana/khata voice apps, scam-call detection, health triage, farmer/village-scheme assistants, voice tutors, PII redaction (already built by someone), voice RPG, voice coding. Bengali/Benglish is rare. Nobody was seen building recipe capture.
