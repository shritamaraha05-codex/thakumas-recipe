## 60-second demo script (Demo Day Drop)
| Time | Shot | Voice/text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 s | Close-up of a handful of rice | "Ek muthi chal." (subtitle: "One fistful of rice. How many grams?") |
| 5-15 s | Thakuma/relative narrating while cooking, transcript appearing | Real Bengali speech, English subtitles |
| 15-27 s | Steps appear with range bars; grain pour shows 33-44 g | "Not 40 g. A range, and we show it." |
| 27-37 s | Weigh the handful, enter grams, band tightens, tag turns "yours" | "Weigh your own hand once." |
| 37-50 s | Cook mode: "porer ta" by voice, timer starts, steam | Hands covered in flour |
| 50-60 s | Recipe card export + one-line result ("coverage X% at width Y, n=Z") + names | Use only measured numbers; if poor, say so |

Rules: show real Gnani calls (Prisma transcript, Timbre voice); subtitles in English; no real third-party private data; consent on screen if a relative appears. Record a fallback take in mock mode.
